Nufa Finance Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Nufa Finance ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use Nufa Finance.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: If you choose Google Sign-In, we may receive your name, email address, and account identifier through Firebase Authentication.
  • Information you enter: Financial, sales, stock, transaction, and other records that you voluntarily enter into the App.
  • Subscription information: Your selected monthly or yearly plan and purchase or entitlement status. Google Play processes payments.
  • Advertising and technical information: Advertising and device information processed by third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob.
  • Support information: Information you provide when contacting us.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain Nufa Finance;
  • To save and synchronize your records when applicable;
  • To provide optional Google Sign-In;
  • To verify subscriptions and provide premium features;
  • To remove advertisements for eligible paid subscribers;
  • To improve security, performance, and reliability; and
  • To respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

3. Google Sign-In and Firebase

Google Sign-In is optional. Users may continue using features available without signing in. If you choose Google Sign-In, authentication may be provided by Firebase Authentication.

4. Advertising

The App may display advertisements through third-party services such as Google AdMob. These services may process device, advertising, and usage information according to their own policies. Eligible paid subscribers may receive an ad-free experience.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Nufa Finance may offer monthly and yearly subscriptions through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment processing. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the App, including authentication, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, and payment services. We may also disclose information when required by law.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain legitimate business purposes, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or comply with legal obligations. To request deletion of your account and associated personal data, contact us at miftahdhe@proton.me. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and accounting purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

Nufa Finance is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
